PROBLEM STATEMENT & IMPACT:
Over 1.4 billion citizens in South Asian megacities face severe chronic particulate and gaseous air pollution without accessible, hyper-localized, or scientifically verified personal mitigation strategies. Existing commercial tools report static AQI indices without explaining what precise actions a parent, commuter, or runner should take. AerisPulse bridges the gap between atmospheric science, epidemiological dosage calculations, and behavioral AI guidance.

KEY INNOVATIONS & ENGINEERING DIFFERENTIATORS:
1. Live Public Telemetry without Mock Data: Connects directly to Open-Meteo European CAMS atmospheric feeds covering PM2.5, PM10, NO2, SO2, O3, CO at 0.1-degree spatial resolution.
2. Automated Multi-Source Anomaly Detection: Cross-references satellite model data with ground regulatory stations, elevating safety alerts when variance exceeds 25%.
3. Deterministic Accounting vs. GenAI Explanation Separation: "The LLM explains; code calculates." Inhaled particulate dosage (ICRP 1994), Chronic Respiratory Exposure Index (Burnett et al. GEMM 2018), and modal commute carbon abatement (DEFRA 2023) are computed deterministically.
4. Zero-Downtime GenAI Resilience: Google Gemini structured JSON output validated with Zod, backed by instant failover to an offline deterministic rules engine if API quotas expire.
5. Grounded Streaming Assistant: Server-Sent Events chat with XML boundary isolation against prompt injections.
6. Multi-Lingual Accessibility: Native English, Tamil (தமிழ்), and Hindi (हिंदी) localization, keyboard navigation, and 0 WCAG 2.2 AA Axe accessibility violations.
7. 181+ Automated Tests: Comprehensive unit, integration, and accessibility test suites passing 100% green with 95.47% line coverage.
